Navni Organic

An online store for Navni Organic, an organic skincare brand.

Role: Sole developer, from design to deployment.

Problem
Navni Organic needed an online store that could bring its skincare products to customers and make the buying process simple, especially on mobile devices.
What I built
I built and launched the store using WooCommerce, with a custom-designed interface created through Elementor and ShopEngine. The website includes dedicated product pages, a shopping cart, and a complete checkout flow, with the experience tested across different devices.
Result
Beyond building the storefront, I made sure the owner could manage the business herself. She can now add and update products and handle customer orders directly through the WooCommerce dashboard.

DESIGN × CODE
